    Context & care

    Writing with attention

    Nüshu is a women’s script associated with Jiangyong County, Hunan, China. It has carried letters, songs, and intimate forms of support across generations.

    The four named women and the moments around them are imagined. Their letters, songs, settings, and social ties are grounded in the historical sources inside each story.

    Nüshu is syllabic: one form may carry the sounds of several Chinese words. The first guide is checked against a documented letter; the other three are clearly marked as provisional dictionary reconstructions.

    This is a living cultural heritage. Enter as a guest, and let each line return your attention to the women and communities that carried it.
